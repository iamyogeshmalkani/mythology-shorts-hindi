# Fact research: yamraj-yamuna

Researched 2026-11-03. Nothing in script, shots or video files was changed.

## (a) Tradition to name aloud

The earliest full text I could open is the **Skanda Purana, Vaishnava-khanda, Karttikamasa-mahatmya, chapter 11 ("The Efficacy of Yamadvitiya")**, on wisdomlib in G. V. Tagare's English translation. It says Yama was once fed by Yamuna at her house on the second day of the bright half of Karttika, and gives a benefit to those who follow the rite. Other Puranic references exist but I could not open them: Padma Purana 6.122.92 (indexed on wisdomlib, 403 error) and a Bhavishya Purana verse "yamo yamunayā pūrvaṃ bhojitaḥ svagṛhe tadā" (seen in a search snippet only). Suggested spoken label: "स्कंद पुराण के कार्तिक माहात्म्य में यम द्वितीया की कथा आती है".

Honest limit: the **tilak on the forehead**, **Yama being too busy**, and the exact wording "अकाल मृत्यु का भय" are NOT in the Skanda chapter I read. They are in popular tradition, calendars and devotional sites. Use "कहा जाता है" or "लोक परंपरा में" for those.

## (d) Open doubts

1. **Padma Purana (6.122.92) and Bhavishya Purana (a verse on Yama fed by Yamuna) are only seen as search-result snippets.** I could not open them. Do not name them aloud until someone reads the verses. Kane's *History of Dharmasastra* (the standard scholarly source) was not found.
2. **The exact boon wording differs by source.** Skanda says "never see the entrance to my abode", "not see the world of Yama even in dreams", and "liberated from premature death". The "no fear of akal mrityu" form is the popular one. Use "कहा जाता है".
3. **Tilak and Yama's busyness** are popular retelling only. Calendars and devotional sites (Sanatan World, the author's tag, was not opened) carry them.
4. **Sanjna as mother is one of several names.** Safe to say "संज्ञा", but "पुराणों के अनुसार" not "वेदों के अनुसार". The Rigveda (10.10) has Yama and Yami as twins in a dialogue where she asks him for a union and he refuses. This is the Vedic Yami, later identified with Yamuna. Do not connect Bhai Dooj to that hymn.
5. **Wikipedia's Yamuna article** notes "some say that she was the daughter of Brahma". Minor, not needed in the video.
6. **Skanda chapter details came through a fetch tool's reading of the English translation.** The verse numbers (18-19, 26-27, 34-36, 50-59) are from that reading; the quoted phrases are from the translation as returned. A human check of Tagare's chapter 11 is advised before publishing.
7. **Rival origin** (Krishna and Subhadra) means the video should not say this is "the" origin of Bhai Dooj.
