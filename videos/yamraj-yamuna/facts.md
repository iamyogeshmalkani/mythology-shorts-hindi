# Fact check: यमराज और यमुना: भाई दूज के पीछे की कहानी

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **Skanda Purana, Vaishnava-khanda, Karttikamasa-mahatmya ch. 11** ("The Efficacy of Yamadvitiya", Tagare translation, wisdomlib): Yamuna feeds Yama at her house on Kartika Shukla Dvitiya; the benefit is release from "premature death" and that one "shall never see the entrance to my abode".
2. **Popular tradition and calendars**: the tilak on the forehead, Yama being too busy to visit, and Yamuna's boon about a brother who receives tilak. Told as "लोककथाओं में कहा जाता है", "लोक परंपरा के अनुसार" and "लोक मान्यता के अनुसार". The Padma and Bhavishya Purana verses could not be opened, so they are not named.
3. A **rival origin** (Krishna visiting Subhadra after killing Narakasura, who applied tilak) is in Wikipedia "Bhai Dooj"; the description mentions it.
