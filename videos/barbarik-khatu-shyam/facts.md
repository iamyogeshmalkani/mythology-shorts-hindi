# Fact check: बर्बरीक: जिसके तीन तीर पूरी महाभारत का युद्ध खत्म कर सकते थे!

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **Barbarika is not in Vyasa's Mahabharata** (Wikipedia: "a folk hero ... not attested in the Mahabharata"). He is in the **Skanda Purana, Kaumarika Khanda chs. 60 (birth) and 66 (death)**, Tagare translation on wisdomlib.
2. **The Khatu Shyam / Rajasthani popular tradition** tells the version in this script: three arrows, the promise to his mother to side with the loser, Krishna as a Brahmin asking for the head, the name Shyam. The Skanda Purana ch. 66 tells it differently: after a boast, Krishna cuts off his head with his discus, and Chandika makes the head immortal. The video says "खाटू श्याम की कथा के अनुसार" in the first line and "लोककथा के अनुसार" / "कथा के अनुसार" at the folk details.
