# Fact research: kumbhakarna-vardan

Researched 2026-11-03. Nothing in script, shots or video files was changed.

## (a) Tradition to name aloud

The video mixes THREE different tellings. Name them, do not blur them:

1. **Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda sarga 10 (7.10)**: the gods ask Brahma not to give Kumbhakarna a real boon; Brahma calls Sarasvati to become the demon's "speech" (vani); Kumbhakarna says "to sleep many years is my wish"; Brahma says "evam astu". No Indra-seat, no six months, no Ravana request here.
2. **Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda sarga 61 (6.61)**: a different account, told by Vibhishana to Rama. Brahma CURSES Kumbhakarna to sleep "as if dead" after the gods complain; Ravana pleads; Brahma fixes "six months asleep, one day awake" (6.61.28).
3. **Popular retellings (not in Valmiki 7.10)**: the Indrasana to Nidrasana slip of the tongue (Sudha Murty, *The Upside-Down King*, 2018, per Wikipedia), and the Nirdevatvam to Nidravatvam wording (Vettam Mani, *Puranic Encyclopaedia*, 1975, which cites "Vana Parva ch. 275 and Uttara Ramayana" for its whole boons section).
4. **Ramcharitmanas (Tulsidas), Balkand, just before doha 177**: "सारद प्रेरि तासु मति फेरी। मागेसि नीद मास षट केरी॥" Saraswati turns his mind and he asks for six months of sleep. No "Indrasan/Nidrasan" there, and no Ravana change.

Suggested spoken label: "वाल्मीकि रामायण और लोकप्रिय कथाओं के अनुसार". The Indrasana/Nidrasana part must be called "लोकप्रिय कथा" (popular retelling), not Valmiki.

## (d) Open doubts

1. **Valmiki is not consistent about the sleep.** 7.10: a sleep-for-years boon via Sarasvati. 7.13.7: he slept "many thousands of years" (bahūnyabdasahasrāṇi). 6.60: Ravana says he sleeps "nine, seven, ten or eight months" (from the English prose page; I did not check the Sanskrit). 6.61.28: six months asleep, one day awake. Saying "Valmiki says six months asleep, one day awake" is safe only with "युद्धकांड में".
2. **Pages are summaries by a fetch tool.** The Sanskrit quotes (7.10.35-40, 6.61.24, 6.61.28) came back verbatim. Statements like "Ravana asks, Brahma answers" in 6.61 and who narrates (Vibhishana) come from the tool's reading of the English page, and the Manas context from its reading of the Hindi page. A human with the Gita Press books should glance at 6.61.25-28 and Balkand 176-177 before publishing.
3. **hindu-blog says Valmiki has "a year asleep, one day awake" and that Ravana never asked for a change.** That contradicts the Sanskrit of 6.61.28 above. The blog cites no verse; do not use it.
4. **Source of Indrasana/Nidrasana unknown.** Only Wikipedia (citing Sudha Murty 2018) and blogs. It may be much older oral tradition; I could not trace it. The author's tag "pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov" was not found or opened.
5. **Mani's "Vana Parva 275" citation** (Ramopakhyana) was not opened, so I cannot say whether the Mahabharata version uses "nirdevatva".
6. **Text edition.** sanskritdocuments hosts the vulgate/Gita Press-type text, not the Baroda critical edition. The 6.61 curse passage and the 7.10 episode may differ in the critical text. Say "वाल्मीकि रामायण (प्रचलित पाठ)" if exactness matters.
7. The user's note "वाल्मीकि: निर्देवत्वम" is NOT supported; the word is not in Valmiki 7.10.
