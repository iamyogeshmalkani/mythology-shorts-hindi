One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
The video mixes **three tellings** and now names each:
1. **Valmiki Ramayana, Uttara Kanda sarga 10 (7.10)**: the gods ask Brahma not to give Kumbhakarna a boon; Brahma himself thinks of Sarasvati, who becomes his speech; Kumbhakarna asks to sleep for many years; Brahma says evam astu.
2. **Valmiki Ramayana, Yuddha Kanda sarga 61 (6.61.28)**: after Ravana pleads, Brahma sets "six months asleep, one day awake". Told as "वाल्मीकि रामायण के युद्धकांड के अनुसार".
3. **Popular retellings**: the Indrasana to Nidrasana slip (Sudha Murty, *The Upside-Down King*, via Wikipedia) and the nirdevatvam to nidravatvam wording (Vettam Mani, *Puranic Encyclopaedia*). Told as "एक लोकप्रिय कथा" and "दूसरा संस्करण".
The Ramcharitmanas (Balkand) has a fourth: Saraswati turns his mind and he asks for six months of sleep.
