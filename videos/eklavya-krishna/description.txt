Mahabharat mein Eklavya Hiranyadhanu ka putra hai, par Harivansh ke anusar woh Krishna ka chachera bhai bhi tha. Janiye yeh rishta kya hai.

Mahabharat ke Adiparv mein Eklavya Nishadraj Hiranyadhanu ka putra hai, jisne Dronacharya ki mitti ki murti ke samne abhyas kiya aur guru dakshina mein apna dahina angootha diya. Harivansh mein uske janm ki ek aur katha milti hai: vahan Devashrava, jo Vasudev ke bhai the, uske pita hain aur Nishadon ne use pala. Is tarah woh Krishna ka chachera bhai thaharta hai.

Yeh katha kahan se hai?
Chachere bhai wali baat Harivansh (Mahabharat ka parishisht mana jane wala granth) mein hai, Mahabharat ke mool Adiparv mein nahi. Brahm Puran mein ek aur roop hai (Shrutdeva ka putra); donon mein sambandh chachere bhai ka banta hai. "chachera" shabd kisi granth mein nahi hai, yeh nishkarsh hai.
