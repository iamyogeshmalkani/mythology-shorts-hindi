# Fact check: एकलव्य: हरिवंश के अनुसार भगवान कृष्ण का चचेरा भाई

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **Vyasa's Mahabharata, Adi Parva Sec. CXXXIV** (Ganguli, ibiblio.org/sripedia m01135): Ekalavya, Hiranyadhanu, Drona's refusal, the clay image, the right thumb.
2. **Harivamsa, Harivamsa Parva ch. 34** (Dutt translation, wisdomlib): Devashrava, brother of Vasudeva, and Ekalavya raised by Nishadas. Told as "हरिवंश के अनुसार". "Cousin" is an inference; no text uses the word, and the Mahabharata calls him Hiranyadhanu's son.

Left out or softened on purpose: the reason Drona refused (the text says he feared a Nishada would outshine his pupils), the dog episode, and any reason why Ekalavya was raised by Nishadas (no source gives one). Wikipedia says Krishna later killed Ekalavya; not in the script, and some viewers may raise it.

Open doubts for a human reviewer: the Dutt Harivamsa follows the vulgate text; verse numbers in the critical edition may differ, so the description names the chapter only.

A person from the tradition should watch the video before it is posted. The check used web sources and the translations named above.
