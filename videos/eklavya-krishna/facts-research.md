# eklavya-krishna: facts research

## (a) Name aloud
- Thumb, Drona's refusal, clay image, Hiranyadhanu: **Vyasa's Mahabharata, Adi Parva (Sambhava Parva), Section CXXXIV** (Ganguli translation).
- Krishna's cousin: **Harivamsa** (the khila, or appendix, of the Mahabharata), **Harivamsa Parva, ch. 34** in the Dutt translation on wisdomlib. Say "हरिवंश के अनुसार", not "एक परंपरा के अनुसार" and not "महाभारत".
- Other Puranas (Vishnu, Brahma) repeat the same lineage with small variants. Many modern retellings treat it as folk, but it is in a named ancient text.

## (d) Open doubts
- The Dutt translation follows the vulgate (Bengal) text. The numbering in the BORI critical edition of the Harivamsa may differ. I could not open a Sanskrit verse (de.wisdomlib.org returned 403). Check the verse in the Sanskrit text before printing a verse number. A chapter number is safe to say as "Harivamsa Parva, ch. 34 (Dutt)".
- **Variants in the Puranas.** Brahma Purana ch. 12 gives "the son of Śrutadevā", the daughter in Shura's list, so Ekalavya would be Krishna's cousin through his father's sister. Vishnu Purana and the Harivamsa say Devashrava. The Vayu Purana is said, in a search snippet from purankosh.in that I did not open, to make Vasudeva the father. "Cousin" holds in either variant, but "Devashrava, Vasudeva's brother" is the Harivamsa/Vishnu version. Name it as the Harivamsa's.
- "Abandoned" (not in the script): the Dutt Harivamsa page I opened does not say abandoned. The Vishnu Purana footnote says he was "exposed as an infant" and the Aitihasika essay says "Nothing is mentioned" about why. Do not add a reason.
- Wikipedia says Krishna later killed Ekalavya (Udyoga Parva reference and Harivamsa). I did not open those passages. If viewers raise it, the "family tie" ending may draw a comment.
- Quotes from ibiblio and wisdomlib came through a summarising fetch tool. Re-read the page before printing a quote in the description.
- The author's tag "wisdomlib.org" checks out: it is the Dutt Harivamsa page above.
