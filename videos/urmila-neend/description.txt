Lokkatha ke anusar, jab Lakshman ne chaudah saal na sone ka sankalp liya, tab unki patni Urmila ne unke hisse ki neend le li.

Urmila Sita ki bahan aur Lakshman ki patni thin. Lokkatha ke anusar, jab Lakshman ne vanvaas mein chaudah saal jagkar Ram aur Sita ki raksha karne ka sankalp liya, tab Nidra devi prakat huin. Lakshman ne apne hisse ki neend kisi aur ko dene ka anurodh kiya aur Urmila ne yeh zimmedari sweekar kar li. Kahte hain ki ve chaudah varshon tak soti rahin.

Yeh katha kahan se hai?
Yeh katha Valmiki Ramayan mein nahi hai. Yeh lokparampara ki katha hai: jaise Telugu mahilaon ke Ramayan-geet ("Urmiladevi Nidra") aur aadhunik punarkathan. Iska sabse purana likhit srot hamein nahi mila. Urmila ka Lakshman ki patni aur Sita ki bahan hona Valmiki Ramayan (Balkand) mein hai.
