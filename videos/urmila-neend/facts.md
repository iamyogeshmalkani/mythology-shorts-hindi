# Fact check: उर्मिला: जिसने लक्ष्मण की जगह 14 साल तक नींद ली

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **Valmiki Ramayana**: Urmila is Janaka's second daughter and Lakshmana's wife (Bala Kanda 1.71, 1.73); the exile is fourteen years (Ayodhya Kanda 2.18); Lakshmana keeps vigil at night (Ayodhya Kanda 51). **The 14-year sleep, Nidra Devi and the boon are not in Valmiki.**
2. **Folk tradition**: the Telugu women's Ramayana songs (the song usually titled *Urmiladevi Nidra*, discussed by V. Narayana Rao in *Manushi* 153, 2006) and folk Ram-kathas (Devdutt Pattanaik); modern retellings (Kavita Kane, *Sita's Sister*, 2014) tell the Nidra scene. No dated earliest source was found. Told as "लोककथा के अनुसार" and "कहते हैं".
