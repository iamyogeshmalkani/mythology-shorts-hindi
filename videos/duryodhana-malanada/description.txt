Kerala ke Kollam mein ek mandir hai jahan Mahabharat ke Duryodhan ki puja hoti hai, aur vahan koi murti tak nahi hai.

Kollam jile ke Poruvazhi gaon mein Peruviruti Malanada mandir hai, jo Duryodhan ko samarpit hai. Yahan na murti hai na garbhgrih: bhakt ek oonche chabutre par man se sankalp karke puja karte hain. Sthaniya manyta hai ki jab Pandav vanvaas mein the, tab unki talash karte hue Duryodhan is ilake mein pahunche the. Yahan toddy chadhane ki parampara hai.

Yeh katha kahan se hai?
Yeh ek sthaniya Kerala katha hai, Mahabharat mein nahi. Iske vivaran mandir ki apni website, samacharon aur yatra-lekhon par aadharit hain; koi shodh-srot hamein nahi mila. Katha ke roop alag-alag hain (kaun unhe pani ya toddy deta hai). Mandir ka pujari kurva samuday se bataya jata hai.
