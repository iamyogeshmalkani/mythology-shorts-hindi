# Fact check: केरल का मंदिर जहाँ दुर्योधन की पूजा होती है

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **The Mahabharata** (epic framing only): Duryodhana is the antagonist.
2. **A local Kerala temple legend** (Poruvazhy Peruviruthy Malanada Temple, Kunnathoor taluk, Kollam), told by the temple's own site and repeated by news and travel sites. It is not in the epic. The script says "स्थानीय परंपरा" and "मान्यता है". Sources are the temple site, Wikipedia, Kerala Tourism, Incredible India, Deccan Chronicle and Curly Tales (journalism and tourism; no scholarly source was found).

Left out or softened on purpose: that toddy is distributed as prasad (no source), "worshipped across India", "the only Duryodhana temple" (not verified; other shrines are said to exist), the festival (Malakkuda Mahotsavam / Kettukazhcha), and who runs the temple (a Kurava priest and an elected committee of seven karas).

Open doubts for a human reviewer: the legend's details vary by source (who gave him water or toddy). All sources are the temple itself, journalism and blogs.
