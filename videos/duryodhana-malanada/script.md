# Kerala ka mandir jahan Duryodhan ki puja hoti hai

Narration is Hindi; this is the same text in Hinglish (as in the captions). The Devanagari text is in `script-devanagari.md`.

Mahabharat ka woh khalnayak, jisne Pandavon ke khilaf yuddh lada tha, Kerala ke ek gaon mein aaj bhi unki puja hoti hai!

Aur is mandir mein unki murti tak nahi hai.

Yeh mandir Kerala ke Kollam jile ke Poruvazhi mein sthit hai.

Iska naam hai Malanada mandir.

Yeh mandir Duryodhan ko samarpit hai.

Aap soch rahe honge ki aakhir jis Duryodhan ko Mahabharat mein adharm ka prateek mana jata hai, uski puja kyun hoti hai?

Sthaneey parampra mein Duryodhan ko ek alag drishtikon se dekha jata hai.

Manyta hai ki jab Pandav vanvaas mein the, tab unki talash karte hue Duryodhan is kshetra mein pahunche the aur yahan ke logon se unka sambandh bana.

Is mandir ki sabse anokhi baat yeh hai ki yahan koi paramprik murti ya garbhgrih nahi hai.

Bhakt ek oonche chabutre par, man se sankalp karke puja karte hain.

Aur sabse hairan karne wali baat?

Yahan Duryodhan ko toddy yani Toddy bhi chadhai jati hai, aur kai bhakt doosri sharab bhi chadhate hain.

Yani Bharat mein ek aisa mandir bhi hai jahan Mahabharat ke sabse vivadit patron mein se ek ki puja hoti hai.

Aapke hisab se Duryodhan sirf ek khalnayak tha ya uski kahani ka koi doosra paksh bhi tha?
