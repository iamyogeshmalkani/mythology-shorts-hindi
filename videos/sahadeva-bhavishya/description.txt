Lokkathaon ke anusar, Sahadev ko Mahabharat ka poora bhavishya pata tha, par ek shraap ke karan ve kisi ko kuch bata nahi sakte the.

Sahadev sabse chhote Pandav the aur apni buddhi ke liye prasiddh the. Dakshin Bharat mein unhe ek kushal jyotishi mana jata hai. Lokkatha ke anusar unhe bhavishya dekhne ki shakti thi, par ek shraap juda tha: kaha jata hai ki agar ve bhavishya kisi ko swayam bata dete, toh unka sir phat jata. Isi karan ve chup rahe.

Yeh katha kahan se hai?
Yeh lokkatha hai. Vyas ke Mahabharat mein Sahadev ke bhavishya-gyaan ya sir phatne ke shraap ka varnan nahi milta (vahan keval gaayon ke bhoot-bhavishya janne ki baat hai, virat parv). Iska sabse purana srot hamein nahi mila. Sabse nazdeeki kshetriya roop Telugu lok-kathaon aur Odia Sarala Mahabharat mein hain, aur ve aapas mein alag hain (Sarala mein poochhe jane par uttar dena padta hai).
