# Fact check: सहदेव: जिसे लोककथाओं में पूरा भविष्य पता था!

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **Vyasa's Mahabharata**: Sahadeva is wise (Mahaprasthanika Parva Sec. 2); his only foreknowledge claim is about cattle (Virata Parva Sec. X). **There is no head-bursting curse and no knowledge of the war's outcome in Vyasa.**
2. **Folk and regional tradition**: the power to see the future and the curse. Told as "लोककथाओं के अनुसार" and "कथा के अनुसार". The earliest source of the curse was not found; the closest regional texts differ (Telugu folk additions: a head-splitting curse; Odia Sarala Mahabharata: a boon that he must answer when asked, and Yudhishthira blames him for silence).
