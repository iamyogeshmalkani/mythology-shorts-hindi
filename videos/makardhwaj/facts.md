# Fact check: मकरध्वज: हनुमान जी का पुत्र, जिसने अपने ही पिता से युद्ध किया!

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **Valmiki Ramayana**: Hanuman burns Lanka (Sundara Kanda ch. 54). **Makardhwaj is not in Valmiki.**
2. **Ananda Ramayana** (a medieval Sanskrit text, Sara Kanda, cited as 1.9.216 on wisdomlib): the birth from a drop of Hanuman's sweat swallowed by a creature of the sea.
3. **Regional Ramayana tellings of the Ahiravana / Mahiravana episode** (Krittivasa's Bengali Ramayana for the Patala kidnapping, the Telugu *Mairavana charitra*, popular retellings): the gatekeeper, the fight and the crowning. **The user's draft credited Krittivasa for Makardhwaj; the Krittivasa episode that was read has no Makardhwaj, so that credit was dropped.** The recognition and the coronation vary by retelling.
