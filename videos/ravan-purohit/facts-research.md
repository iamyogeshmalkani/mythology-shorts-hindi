# ravan-purohit: fact research

Researched 2026-11-03. Research only; script.md, shots and video files were not touched.

## (a) What to name aloud

- Name it as a **popular retelling / oral folk story attached to Rameswaram**, not a Shaiva text and not Valmiki. The honest phrase is "एक लोकप्रिय लोककथा / कहानी, जो रामेश्वरम से जुड़ी बताई जाती है".
- The only named "source" I found for the Ravana-as-priest story is a text called the **"Veda Vyasa Ramayana"**, cited by two modern web articles (The Daily Star, Bangladesh, 2009, and an Advaita-l mailing-list post, 2019). I could not identify or open that text. It is not a standard critical-edition Ramayana.
- What the real texts say: Rameswaram's Shiva worship by Rama appears in the **Shiva Purana (Koti Rudra Samhita 31)**, where Rama himself makes a sand (parthiva) lingam, and Shiva grants victory. Valmiki Yuddha 6.123 has Rama saying Shiva formerly "bestowed his grace on me" at the Setu. Neither text has Ravana as priest.
- Do NOT say Kamba Ramayanam, Telugu or Tamil classical text, or Valmiki tells it. I found no support.
- Do NOT call it a "शैव परंपरा" (Shaiva tradition): the Shaiva text (Shiva Purana) does not include the Ravana-priest scene. That word in s4 overstates.

## (d) Open doubts

- No classical or scholarly text was found for the Ravana-priest story. Kamba Ramayanam, Jaiminiya, Telugu and Tamil retellings were searched; none was shown to contain it. A forum post (search snippet) suggested Kamban does not mention the lingam installation, which is weak evidence. I could not read Kamban's Setu Bandhana section, so "absent from Kamba" is NOT confirmed.
- The "Veda Vyasa Ramayana" cited by the Daily Star and Advaita-l is unidentified. It may be a modern or regional compilation. Do not repeat the name as an authority.
- The author's source tag (The New Indian Express, 13.05.2024) was not found. The site was blocked for my search tool and I did not open it. Treat it as UNVERIFIED.
- The Art of Living Q&A (Vibhishana suggests Ravana; Rama sends an invitation) was seen only in a search snippet, not opened. Devotional source.
- Versions differ on details the script omits: in the Advaita-l version Ravana says Rama cannot install the idol without his wife, brings Sita for the puja on condition she returns, and asks as dakshina that Rama be with him at his death. The script's silence on these is fine, but a viewer who knows that version may expect it.
- Valmiki: Yuddha 6.123 and 6.125 have no priest scene. This is limited to the chapters opened, not a full-text search.
- Quotes passed through a summarising fetch tool; re-check them on the live pages before printing.
