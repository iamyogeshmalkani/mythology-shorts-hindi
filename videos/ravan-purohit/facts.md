# Fact check: रावण: जिसने अपने ही शत्रु श्रीराम की पूजा करवाई!

One row per claim in `script.md`. Checked 2026-11-03 (research report: `facts-research.md`, done by a research agent and spot-checked). `node scripts/check-facts.mjs <project>` must pass before `voice`. **`verified` here means: read in the named source, and the script tells it as that source or tradition tells it.** Folk and regional stories are told as such.

**Traditions kept apart.**
1. **Not in Valmiki, and not in the Shaiva text either**: the Shiva Purana (Koti Rudra Samhita 31) has Rama make a sand lingam himself, with no Ravana; Valmiki Yuddha 6.123 has only Rama saying Shiva favoured him at the Setu. **No classical or scholarly source for Ravana as priest was found.**
2. **A popular modern retelling attached to Rameswaram.** Modern web articles tell it (The Daily Star 2009; an Advaita-l mailing list post, 2019; Asianet Newsable), and two of them cite an unidentified "Veda Vyasa Ramayana". Told as "एक ऐसी कथा भी प्रचलित है", "लोकप्रिय लोककथा", "कथा के अनुसार", "कहा जाता है". The user's draft called it a "Shaiva tradition"; that was not supported and was removed.
**This is the weakest-sourced of the nine stories.** Treat it as a popular tale only.
