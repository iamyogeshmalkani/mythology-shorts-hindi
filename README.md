## What is in each folder

`video.mp4` (web-compressed) · `captions.srt` / `captions.vtt` (Hindi) · `cover.jpg` · `thumbnail-a.jpg`, `thumbnail-b.jpg` (1280x720) · `title.txt` · `description.txt` · `PACKAGE.md` (6 title options, tags, hashtags, pinned comment, poll, upload checklist) · `THUMBNAIL-BRIEF.md` · `metadata.json` (all of it, machine-readable) · `script.md` · `notes.md` (edits to the original script) · `facts.md` and `facts-research.md` (claim-by-claim check, with sources).

## Read this before posting

- **AI content.** The narration is an AI voice clone and the pictures are AI-generated. YouTube asks whether the video contains altered or synthetic content; answer it yourself (turning it on is the safer choice for a cloned voice). The voice reference recording is **not** in this repository.
- **Tradition, not text.** Most of these are folk or regional retellings. Each video says which tradition it follows, and `facts.md` shows what was found in the epics and Puranas. `ravan-purohit` is the weakest sourced: no classical source was found for Ravana as Rama's priest.
- **Not yet reviewed by a person from the tradition,** and the narration and music were not listened to by the maker. Please watch before posting.
- Captions are the narration text; the speech model's word timing was checked only automatically.
- Made with [explainroo](https://github.com/vincentsch/explainroo) (the `myth-facts` template, Viraj F5 voice clone, Perchance pictures). No licence has been added, so all rights are reserved until you add one.
